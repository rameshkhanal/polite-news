Font Awesome 4.7.0
by Dave Gandy - http://fontawesome.io

This theme bundles the Font Awesome icon font files (fontawesome-webfont.eot,
.svg, .ttf, .woff, .woff2 and FontAwesome.otf) and the Font Awesome stylesheet
(assets/css/font-awesome.css and font-awesome.min.css).
